Statement of Commitment


PAX_API_GATEWAY is a component of the Anticloud sovereign AI infrastructure. As part of the Anticloud FZ LLE ecosystem, this project carries explicit commitments to the societies in which it operates and those affected by its deployment.


Principles


Transparency Over Opacity

PAX_API_GATEWAY operates with a SHA3-256 AIOSS append-only audit chain, ensuring every inference, deployment event, and data operation is cryptographically logged. Societies interacting with systems built on PAX_API_GATEWAY can demand and receive verifiable audit records — not a vendor's word, but mathematical proof.


Sovereignty Before Convenience

This project is designed for on-premise, air-gap capable deployment. Communities and nations should not be forced to route sensitive data through external cloud providers to access AI capabilities. PAX_API_GATEWAY makes sovereign deployment the default, not the exception.


Regulated-Domain Access

In the PAX 27B model inference, Q4 quantization, transformer architecture space, PAX_API_GATEWAY specifically unlocks AI access for regulated sectors — healthcare, government, finance, defense — where cloud AI is structurally prohibited. Access to AI should not require waiving legal and safety protections.


No Dark Patterns

PAX_API_GATEWAY ships under Apache 2.0 and the Anticommons Enterprise License 1.0. No proprietary lock-in. No hidden telemetry. No data extracted to Anticloud servers without explicit consent.
