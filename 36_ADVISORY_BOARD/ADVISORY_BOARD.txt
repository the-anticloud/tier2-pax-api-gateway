## Why Ex-Frontier Lab Advisors

### The Signal Value

When an ex-OpenAI research scientist or ex-Google DeepMind engineer joins as advisor, it communicates:
1. **Technical legitimacy** — insiders with access to proprietary benchmarks and internal architecture reviews choose to associate with this
2. **Market intelligence** — they know what the frontier labs cannot build due to business model constraints
3. **Customer introductions** — they have Rolodexes of enterprise AI procurement contacts from their previous roles
4. **Investor signal** — top-tier VCs track where frontier lab alumni go. Advisor announcements generate inbound.

### The Discount Opportunity

Ex-frontier lab employees typically leave for one of three reasons:
1. **Equity cliff:** They vested and left before the next big grant
2. **Mission misalignment:** They wanted to build AI for access/sovereignty, not for API revenue
3. **Bureaucracy:** Large org, slow decisions, wanted to move faster

These are our target profiles. They are technically credible, available, and often ideologically aligned with sovereign AI.
