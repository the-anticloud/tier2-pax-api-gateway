## Why a budget rather than a log

Preprioception is the claim that a cost can be known before the act
that incurs it. If every act is a gesture (ETHICS/01), then each gesture
carries a cost, and the only difference between acting and gambling is
whether that cost was declared first. That the link to preprioception is
our synthesis rather than a claim by Lois-Kleinner Alpasan is recorded
in ETHICS/01; it is a design position and is labelled as one.
