## Environmental Position

Anticloud FZ LLE is building AI infrastructure that is structurally more environmentally efficient than the cloud AI status quo. `PAX_API_GATEWAY` contributes to this mission.

## Energy Efficiency

**Compute Footprint**
The Anticloud PAX L5 Narrow L2 General 27B model — which `PAX_API_GATEWAY` supports — runs on a single T4 GPU at 70W TDP, consuming approximately $0.002/hr in electricity at UAE commercial rates. Compare this to training and serving large cloud frontier models requiring thousands of H100 GPUs and datacenter-scale cooling.

**On-Premise vs. Cloud**
Cloud AI routes data across continents through energy-intensive hyperscale datacenters. `PAX_API_GATEWAY` enables on-premise deployment, eliminating redundant data transmission energy costs and allowing organizations to use locally sourced or renewable power.

**Quantization as Efficiency**
Q4 quantization of the PAX 27B model reduces memory footprint by ~75% versus FP16, directly translating to lower VRAM requirements, lower power draw, and the ability to run on existing hardware rather than requiring new silicon.

## Carbon Accounting

Anticloud commits to documenting the compute cost of every model run via the AIOSS chain. Each logged inference includes hardware fingerprint and token count, enabling accurate carbon accounting for organizations with ESG reporting requirements.

## Scope 3 Emissions

By reducing dependency on cloud AI data transmission and enabling deployment in energy-efficient edge and on-premise environments, `PAX_API_GATEWAY` helps downstream organizations reduce their scope 3 emissions related to AI workloads.
