## The canon

> "Epistemic Gesturalism is the acknowledgement that time, our existence
> and for as long and far or short and wide we can percieve is just rthe
> sam gesture, played dcifferently, and played in different gestures across
> time, everybody is acting onlyu gestures, it is all a gesture-- even our
> existence itself is but a gesture"

-- Lois-Kleinner Alpasan, as supplied by the project owner

## What is canon and what is ours

The quoted statement is canon. The three consequences below are **our**
reading, not the author's, and are labelled so wherever they are reused:

1. **Cost follows gesture.** If an act is a gesture, it is not free. This
   is the warrant for a declared budget rather than a spend log.
2. **Knowing before acting.** Preprioception is meaningful because the
   cost of a gesture can be estimated before it is made.
3. **Accounting is the point.** A gesture that leaves no trace cannot be
   later distinguished from one that did not happen, so the ledger is not
   an audit convenience but a precondition for the claim.

The spelling in the quotation is preserved as supplied. It has not been
silently corrected, because a canon that is quietly edited is no longer a
canon; correction is a decision for the author, not for the corpus.
