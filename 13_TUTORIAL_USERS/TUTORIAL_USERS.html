Getting Started as an End User


This tutorial explains how to use PAX_API_GATEWAY if you are an end user — someone accessing AI capabilities through a system built on Anticloud infrastructure. You do not need to be a programmer.


What PAX_API_GATEWAY Does for You


PAX_API_GATEWAY is part of the Anticloud sovereign AI infrastructure. When your organization deploys it, you get:


  • AI that stays on your organization's premises — your data never leaves your control
  • A cryptographic receipt for every AI interaction — you can verify what happened
  • Compliance with HIPAA, GDPR, FedRAMP — the AI is safe to use with regulated data

    • Using the Interface


    Depending on how your organization has deployed PAX_API_GATEWAY, you may access it through:


    1. Web interface — A browser-based chat or document interface

    2. API — For developers building on top of the deployment

    3. Command line — For technical users


    Asking Questions


    When you submit a question or document to PAX_API_GATEWAY:


    1. Your input is processed locally on your organization's servers

    2. The AI generates a response using PAX L5 Narrow L2 General 27B

    3. A cryptographic log entry is created (you can request this for audit purposes)

    4. The response is returned to you


    Data Privacy


  • Your data does not leave your organization's servers
  • No data is sent to Anticloud FZ LLE unless you explicitly share it
  • You can request a copy of your audit log at any time
