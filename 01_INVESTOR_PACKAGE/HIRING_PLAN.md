## Strategic Premise

The AI labor market in 2025–2026 is structurally dislocated. Frontier labs, F-500 technology divisions, and AI startups have executed waves of delayering — cost-cutting headcount reductions that have put world-class ML engineers, AI safety researchers, and infrastructure engineers onto the market at significant compensation discounts from their prior packages. These are not underperformers. They are highly credentialed, experienced, and actively looking for environments that are technically serious and financially stable.

Anticloud's hiring strategy targets this specific pool: ex-frontier lab (Meta, Google DeepMind, OpenAI, Anthropic, Oracle AI) and F-500 AI division talent, acquired at structural market discount, retained through competitive long-term compensation, equity alignment, and an environment that eliminates the reasons people hop.

**The core insight:** a 30% engineering allocation on an $80M raise ($24M) can acquire and retain a world-class 12–15 person team for 36+ months if structured correctly. The goal is not headcount — it is capability density and retention.

---

## Retention Philosophy

High turnover in AI teams is not primarily a compensation problem. It is an environment problem. Engineers at frontier labs leave because:
1. Bureaucracy and org politics slow technical progress
2. Their work ships into a blackbox product they cannot publish or claim
3. The mission has drifted from research to product shipping under commercial pressure
4. Lack of ownership, credit, and attribution

Anticloud's retention strategy addresses each directly:
- Flat structure: no middle management layers between engineer and founder
- Open corpus: all 123 projects are publicly attributed, citable, and ORCID-linked
- Mission coherence: the technical agenda (proprioceptive AI, AIOSS, SoT) is the product — there is no drift between research and deployment
- Long retainer contracts with milestone bonuses: 24–36 month agreements with performance-linked upside, reducing incentive to hop
- Competitive base + equity pool: OTE at or above frontier lab levels, not discounted because of market conditions

**The structural discount is captured at hire; the retention is purchased through environment and long-term contracts, not through underpaying and hoping people stay.**
