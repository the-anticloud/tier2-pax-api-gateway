## Why AI-Specific Insurance Matters

Deploying AI in regulated sectors creates novel liability surfaces:

1. **Inference liability** — If an AI recommendation causes harm (clinical, financial, legal), who is liable?
2. **Audit trail liability** — If an audit trail is incomplete or corrupted, who bears the compliance risk?
3. **Data breach liability** — If the on-premise deployment is compromised, what is the coverage?
4. **IP infringement liability** — If the model reproduces training data verbatim, what coverage applies?
